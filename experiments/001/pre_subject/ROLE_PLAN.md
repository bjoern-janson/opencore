## Seed custody boundary

The 64 evaluation-seed values and decryption passphrase must remain outside the
repository, Codex, CI, subject developer tools, synchronized storage, telemetry,
logs, and shell history until the reveal gate. Only the public commitment
register may enter the preimplementation lineage. The encrypted reveal bundle
and passphrase remain in separate protected custody.

The helper source cannot prove externality, independent sampling, secrecy, or
no replacement. The custodian declares those facts; the public commitment fixes
the later-revealed value; Phase 0 and the attack adjudicator decide whether the
custody evidence is adequate.

No evaluation arm may run before reveal. No result may be inserted into a
prospective artifact. Loss or premature disclosure yields
