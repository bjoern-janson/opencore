## What PCE-001 earned

On this frozen learned organism/environment family:

1. persistent reveal learning completely suppressed a prospectively identified corrective probe after that probe had previously been costly;
2. same-state counterfactuals showed that correction-relevant opportunities existed almost immediately after the exogenous shift;
3. cutting the state-to-challenge-timing edge with a state-independent hazard caused correction in `195/256` seeds where the endogenous arm corrected in `0/256`;
4. the paired censored correction-latency difference was large and in the prospectively predicted direction;
5. strictly positive but vanishing policy support was materially weaker than a nonvanishing quantitative exposure floor;
6. the same intervention was costly in a nondiscriminating world and merely informative in an action-irrelevant world.

The strongest scoped statement is therefore:

> **In this frozen learned adaptive system, persistent state endogenously eliminated exposure to a known corrective channel. Directly restoring state-independent exposure changed later correction dynamics, while matched null and informative controls showed that the result was not equivalent to generic probing or generic information acquisition.**

This upgrades the lineage from observed challenge suppression to a causally localized endogenous evidence-acquisition failure in the tested family.
