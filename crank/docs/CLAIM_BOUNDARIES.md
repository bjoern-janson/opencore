# Claim Boundaries and Current Status

## 1. Why this document exists

OpenCore deliberately separates:

```text
observed result
candidate interpretation
future target
architectural metaphor
```

This page keeps those authority levels explicit.

## 2. Experimentally supported on constructed families

### Mini core lifecycle

Supported on the toy affine family:

- finite-hypothesis acquisition;
- persistence and reload;
- composition of learned scopes;
- local reopening/revision;
- probe request when replacement is underdetermined;
- preservation of unaffected scope in tested revisions.

### M2-style intervention diagnosis

Supported on constructed interface families:

- representation repair can differ from acquiring a missing distinction;
- finite history can leave interface adequacy unresolved.

### M3/M4 closure separation

Supported on constructed adversary families:

```text
model support != closure permission
closure permission != truth guarantee
```

### M5 reopening

Supported on constructed typed post-closure events:

```text
reopenability != panic
```

### M6 recursive closure grounding

Supported narrowly:

> given externally grounded provenance identifying evidence as bearing on the closure mechanism, Mini can change a future closure policy and preserve that correction under the tested optimization pressure.

### R1 transfer discrimination

Supported narrowly:

> given two supplied opaque transfer hypotheses and a finite candidate context set, uncertainty over transfer structure can drive a discriminating probe.

### Nano V0

with two preserved pre-repair failures.

### Mini × Nano Composition V0

## 3. Candidate common interpretations

These have explanatory support but are not established universal laws.

### Lawful adaptive transition

Candidate:

> an adaptive transition should neither erase correction-relevant distinctions nor manufacture authority not warranted upstream.

### Premature quotient / unauthorized refinement

Candidate dual failure family:

```text
premature quotient
unauthorized refinement
```

### Reliable generalization as warranted invariance

Candidate target:

```text
reliable generalization = discovery and governance of warranted invariance
```

Equivalent useful view:

```text
reliable generalization ~= warranted quotient construction
```

## 4. Mental models, not scientific claims

### "Tiny computational world" / "mini planet"

Useful architecture analogy:

```text
Nano = persistence/world rules
Mini = adaptive organism
Base = larger world + adaptive processes + their interaction rules
```

This is a conceptual aid, not evidence that a general computational world architecture has been solved.

### "Authority firewall"

Useful operational description of Nano's current role:

> a boundary between adaptive proposal and persistent state that can reject effects exceeding supplied contracts.

Nano is not a firewall against false beliefs or bad reasoning.

## 6. Formal E001 separation

Nothing in the crank lineage constitutes:

- E001 subject implementation completion;
- E001 development qualification;
- E001 evaluation execution;
- E001 evaluation evidence;
- E001 seed reveal;
- modification of K0.

The formal seed-custody gate remains independent and must not be bypassed by crank experimentation.

## 7. Current research posture

The working standard is:

> preserve access to interesting hypotheses without granting them authority they have not earned.

That applies to successful results as much as failed ones.

The right next experiment is therefore the one that best discriminates competing explanations of the current results, not the one that makes the architecture look more complete.
