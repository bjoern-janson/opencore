# Developmental Theory — Corrective Exposure and Persistent Authority

## Status

**Status:** `SYSTEMS_LEVEL_HYPOTHESIS_FREEZE`  
**Scientific authority:** conceptual compression grounded by earlier constructed results, not a demonstrated full developmental loop  
**Kernel change:** none

This document freezes the current systems-level compression so the next advance can be empirical rather than conceptual.

## 1. The central chain

OpenCore currently separates:

```text
Reality
-> challenge / apparatus
-> consequence
-> organism interpretation
-> candidate transition
-> persistent authority
```

The load-bearing distinction is:

```text
reality != measurement != interpretation != authority
```

Reality does not enter the computational system directly. An apparatus creates a channel through which some consequence becomes observable. The organism interprets that consequence and proposes a transition. The persistence substrate constrains whether that proposal may acquire durable authority.

Nothing in this chain implies that the apparatus is complete, the interpretation is correct, or the external transition contract is epistemically sufficient.

## 2. Division of labor

The current systems picture is:

```text
Reality   constrains
Apparatus makes some constraint observable
Mini      proposes, probes, interprets, and revises
Nano      constrains persistent authority transitions
Base      constitutes the currently effective shared computational world
```

A useful agency/authority compression is:

> **The organism chooses where to risk being wrong.**
>
> **The persistence world constrains what the resulting consequence is allowed to change.**

This intentionally preserves:

```text
freedom to generate != freedom to canonize
```

Nano is not a challenge selector and is not a truth oracle.

## 3. Developmental claim

The current systems-level hypothesis is:

> **Intelligence develops by constructing increasingly useful ways for reality to correct what currently governs it.**

The target of correction is broader than a predictive model. Currently governing structure may include:

- a hypothesis;
- a transfer relation;
- a closure rule;
- an acquisition policy;
- a challenge policy;
- a representation;
- a shared Base invariant;
- another persistent authority-bearing mechanism.

This is not yet an experimentally demonstrated general theory of intelligence.

## 4. Developmental loop

A compact loop is:

```text
A_t -> F_t -> C_t -> E_t -> I_t -> Delta A_{t+1}
```

where:

```text
A_t              currently effective authority structure
F_t              frontier of discriminable uncertainty
C_t              selected challenge / apparatus intervention
E_t              observed consequence
I_t              organism interpretation / candidate transition
Delta A_{t+1}    warranted persistent authority change
```

Then `A_{t+1}` changes which future frontiers and challenges become available.

The loop is therefore recursively developmental rather than merely accumulative.

## 5. Frontier of discriminable uncertainty

The useful operational compression is:

```text
live alternatives
+
feasible discriminating challenge
+
recoverable consequence
+
live authority-update path
```

This is deliberately more specific than an "edge of chaos" metaphor.

Too little challenge can produce high success with little corrective information.

Too much unconstrained difficulty can produce failure with poor attribution and little usable learning.

The desired regime is **structured surprise**: multiple live possibilities plus feasible consequence that can separate them and lawfully alter what currently governs.

A challenge can be highly informative while having little corrective leverage if no legitimate authority-update path exists.

Thus:

```text
information gain != corrective leverage
```

## 6. Epistemic bravery

A useful organism-level hypothesis is:

> **Epistemic bravery is maintaining genuine corrective exposure of currently authoritative structure.**

This does not mean maximizing danger, difficulty, novelty, or failure rate.

A tiny probe can be highly corrective if its consequence can lawfully reopen an important invariant. A dramatic exploration can have low corrective value if nothing observed can reduce the authority of the assumptions that govern the system.

The prospective challenge-selection problem is therefore not simply:

```text
choose the hardest task
```

but approximately:

```text
seek challenges where failure is possible,
discriminating,
attributable,
recoverable,
and connected to a lawful authority-update path
```

Challenge selection remains organism-level behavior, not a Nano responsibility.

## 7. Global reopening and world plasticity

Base-001 and Base-002 motivate a shared-world view in which local cognition is conditioned on authority-effective persistent state.

A useful conceptual object is:

```text
S_effective = authority-filtered current world
```

with heterogeneous local projections:

```text
W_i = P_i(S_effective, h_i)
```

A warranted shared-world change can therefore influence many local projections without copying an irreversible update into every unit.

When upstream warrant authority is withdrawn, dependent consequences can cease to be effective without destructive replay through all local states.

This motivates the phrase:

> **warrant-mediated world plasticity**

and the narrower Base capability:

> **global reopening without global replacement**

Base-002A demonstrated the target-bound constructed path:

```text
G effective
-> counterexample e*
G reopened
-> distributed uncertainty
-> later independent evidence E'
G' installed
```

The same counterexample-backed reopen capability was not allowed to install `G'`.

The constructed result therefore separates:

```text
ADMIT(G) != REOPEN(G) != REPLACE(G, G')
```

and:

```text
refutation authority != replacement authority
```

## 8. Guard versus warrant parent

Base-002B exposed a Nano V0 negative result.

A natural strict reopening contract wants to require:

```text
G_STATUS = EFFECTIVE
```

as an execution condition before reopening `G`.

Nano V0 currently promotes every satisfied precondition source into a continuing warrant parent of the resulting receipt. The strict reopen transition therefore makes the new `REOPENED` standing depend on the old `G` standing whose authority the same transition revokes.

The result self-invalidates.

The forced distinction is:

```text
Guard(T) !=> WarrantParent(Result(T))
```

or:

> **A fact may be required for a transition to occur without being something the resulting authority should continue to depend on.**

This is distinct from the earlier Nano distinction:

```text
preservation obligation != warrant dependency
```

No repair representation is frozen here. Typed guards, explicit warrant-parent declarations, or another smaller mechanism remain competing possibilities.

## 9. Corrective exposure is not guaranteed correction

The systems hypothesis must retain:

```text
corrective exposure != guaranteed correction
```

Failure remains possible at every layer:

- apparatus may be biased, lossy, or non-identifying;
- consequence may be ambiguous;
- organism interpretation may be wrong;
- challenge selection may be timid, reckless, or self-confirming;
- contracts may encode the wrong epistemic distinctions;
- Nano can enforce a supplied contract without establishing that the contract is correct.

OpenCore therefore does not claim to ensure truth.

Its prospective purpose is narrower:

> **Preserve lawful pathways by which reality can reduce the authority of structures that no longer deserve to govern.**

## 10. Current stopping boundary

This compression is frozen as a hypothesis, not promoted to a kernel primitive or a completed architecture.

The next useful advance should be empirical: construct a case that discriminates or breaks some part of this loop rather than elaborating the theory aesthetically.
